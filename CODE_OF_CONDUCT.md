# Code of conduct

AMUN is a hobbyist project that welcomes anyone who wants to build or use achievements for
their 3DS games. To keep it friendly:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers. Not everyone has the same console, skills or time.
- Keep discussion on topic: the tools, the sets, and the games they cover.
- No piracy. Do not ask for, share or link to pirated games, keys or firmware in project spaces.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through the
repository's private reporting channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
